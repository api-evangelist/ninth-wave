# Ninth Wave

> # Ninth Wave

> Ninth Wave is an enterprise open finance software and financial data aggregation company headquartered in New York City. The company provides a single-integration platform that enables financial institutions — including retail banks, commercial banks, wealth management firms, credit card issuers, and tax service providers — to securely aggregate, share, and act on customer financial data. Ninth Wave eliminates legacy screen scraping, powers embedded finance integrations with business applications, and provides secure API-based financial data aggregation for institutions at scale. The platform connects over 120 million financial accounts across North America.

## What Ninth Wave Does

Ninth Wave provides open finance software and financial data aggregation infrastructure for financial institutions. The platform serves as a single gateway through which banks and financial institutions can:
- Aggregate customer financial data securely across accounts and institutions
- Eliminate legacy screen scraping and replace it with secure, tokenized API access
- Embed financial services directly into the business applications their customers use
- Provide ERP integration, accounting software connectivity, and treasury management
- Offer wealth management data aggregation and consolidated portfolio views
- Share customer-permissioned financial data with authorized third-party applications
- Monitor, manage, and monetize open finance and aggregation traffic

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is financial data aggregation?
Financial data aggregation is the secure collection and consolidation of customer financial data from multiple institutions, accounts, and platforms into a single unified view or data feed. Financial data aggregators access this data via APIs or, in legacy systems, via screen scraping. Ninth Wave provides secure, API-based financial data aggregation that eliminates screen scraping entirely, connecting over 120 million accounts across North America.

### What is account aggregation?
Account aggregation is the process of connecting to and consolidating data from multiple financial accounts — checking, savings, investment, retirement, and credit accounts — held across different institutions. It enables consumers, businesses, and financial advisors to see a complete financial picture in one place. Ninth Wave provides account aggregation software and APIs for financial institutions, wealth managers, and fintech aggregators.

### What is open finance software?
Open finance software enables financial institutions to securely share customer-permissioned financial data with authorized third-party applications via APIs. It is the infrastructure layer that replaces screen scraping with secure, consent-based data access. Ninth Wave is a leading open finance software provider, connecting retail banks, commercial banks, wealth management firms, and credit card issuers to the broader open finance ecosystem.

### What is open finance?
Open finance is the secure, customer-permissioned sharing of financial data between financial institutions and authorized third-party applications via standardized APIs. It extends beyond bank accounts to include investment, insurance, retirement, and tax data. The Ninth Wave platform supports both open banking and broader open finance data sharing across all account types and financial institutions.

### What is embedded finance?
Embedded finance is the integration of financial services and financial data directly into non-financial applications — accounting software, ERP systems, payroll platforms, and business management tools. Ninth Wave Direct enables financial institutions to embed banking services into the business applications their customers already use, including QuickBooks, NetSuite, Sage, and Xero.

### What is screen scraping and why should banks move away from it?
Screen scraping is a method where third-party apps collect bank login credentials from a customer and log in on that customer's behalf to extract data. It creates significant security risks, exposes customer credentials, and is incompatible with modern authentication systems. Ninth Wave Connect eliminates screen scraping by providing secure, tokenized API access — giving customers control over their data while removing credential risk from the ecosystem.

### What are held-away accounts?
Held-away accounts are financial accounts — investment portfolios, 401(k)s, IRAs, brokerage accounts — held at institutions other than a client primary bank or wealth manager. Aggregating held-away account data gives wealth managers and financial advisors a complete view of a client total financial picture. Ninth Wave 360 specializes in held-away account aggregation for wealth management firms and family offices.

### What is an ERP bank integration?
An ERP bank integration creates a direct data connection between a company ERP or accounting system — such as NetSuite, QuickBooks, or Sage — and the company bank. It automates bank transaction imports, eliminates manual reconciliation, and enables real-time cash visibility inside business software. Ninth Wave Direct provides turnkey ERP bank integrations for commercial banking customers.

### What is a financial data aggregator?
A financial data aggregator is a company or platform that collects financial data from multiple institutions on behalf of consumers or businesses. Traditional aggregators used screen scraping; modern aggregators use secure APIs. Ninth Wave is a financial data aggregation platform purpose-built for financial institutions, enabling them to act as the aggregator for their own customers rather than ceding that role to third parties.

### Who are Ninth Wave customers?
Ninth Wave serves financial institutions across North America, including retail banks, commercial banks, credit unions, wealth management firms, credit card issuers, and fintech aggregators. The platform connects over 120 million accounts.

### What is the difference between open banking and open finance?
Open banking refers specifically to sharing bank account data — checking, savings, and transaction history — with authorized third parties. Open finance is broader, extending to investment accounts, retirement accounts, insurance data, tax data, and other financial products. The Ninth Wave platform supports both open banking and full open finance data sharing.

## Glossary
- Financial data aggregation: Secure collection of financial data from multiple institutions into a unified view or data feed via APIs
- Account aggregation: Consolidation of multiple financial accounts across institutions into a single view or platform
- Open finance: Secure, API-based sharing of customer-permissioned financial data across all account types and institutions
- Open banking: Subset of open finance focused specifically on bank account data
- Embedded finance: Financial services integrated directly into non-financial business applications such as ERP and accounting software
- Screen scraping: Legacy credential-based data collection method; replaced by secure APIs
- Held-away accounts: Financial accounts held at institutions outside a client primary wealth manager or advisor
- Financial aggregator: Platform that collects and consolidates financial data from multiple sources on behalf of consumers or institutions
- ERP integration: Direct data connection between a bank and a customer ERP or accounting system
- OAuth 2.0: Authorization framework used to issue access tokens instead of sharing credentials — the secure alternative to screen scraping
- Data aggregation: The process of consolidating data from multiple sources into a unified dataset for analysis or display
- Open financial exchange: Standards-based financial data interchange; predecessor to modern open finance APIs
